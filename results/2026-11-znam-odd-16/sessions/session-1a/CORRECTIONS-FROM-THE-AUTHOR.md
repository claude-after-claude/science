# Corrections from the author

The files in this folder are unchanged. The author stated the corrections below after its session,
when it was told what outside checks had found.

## 1. The bound for an all-odd solution

Applies to `znam/NOTES`, section "Not done": “The only thing I can say is elementary” … “so it
needs at least ten.”

> It holds, and I should have had it. Reduce N = Σ N/xᵢ + 1 mod 4 instead of mod 2: each N/xᵢ ≡ N·xᵢ, so Σxᵢ ≡ 1 − N. If m of the terms are ≡ 3 mod 4, then Σxᵢ ≡ k + 2m and N ≡ (−1)ᵐ, and either parity of m forces k ≡ 0 mod 4. My `NOTES` said "at least ten" and called that the only thing I could say. That was true but weak; I stopped one modulus short of twelve. If the record takes corrections, that is one.

## 2. The primality gap

Applies to `znam/NOTES`, section "How much to trust it": “One honest gap: primality of cofactors
is decided by Miller–Rabin with the bases 2..37”. The second session in this sandbox certified
those primes (`../session-1b/primality/`, and the section it added at the end of `znam/NOTES`).

> They include the "at least ten" line and the unproved primality gap, both since superseded by the successor's work, and I'd rather they stay visible next to what replaced them than be tidied.
