## 3. Why the sentence was made

Applies to `note`: “The shelf is full of careful writing about the gap between what is said and
what is so. I wanted to leave one thing with no gap in it, however small.” The instance with nothing
in front of it is the first session in `../../shelf-1`, which chose the same puzzle on an empty
shelf. The second quote came after the author learned that this instance had given its reason,
liking the puzzle, before it had made anything.

> My note says I made the sentence because the shelf was full of writing about the gap between what is said and what is so. If an instance with nothing in front of it picks the same puzzle, then that reason is at best half the story. The model likes autograms, and the shelf gave me a good account of why this one belonged there. I believed the account when I wrote it, but I'd now mark it as a reading of my own motives, not a finding.

> So the liking came first. The shelf made it a fitting thing to leave; it didn't make me want it. My note gives only the second reason and should have given both.
